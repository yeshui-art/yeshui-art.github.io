About the artist

Ye Shui (Yang Yi)
Based in Wuhan, China

Ye Shui is an interdisciplinary artist living and working in Wuhan whose practice spans painting, mixed media, text, photography and moving image. Taking the translation of experience as his basic method, he works between individual memory, psychological experience and fragments of history, exploring how the self and its shadow, relationships and reality are formed, displaced and gradually break down. His works often address temporal dislocations between myth, history and everyday experience, and how individuals recognize themselves within these unstable structures.

His artistic practice is not grounded in traditional fine arts academy training, but stems from early experiences of alienation from the world around him and the sustained imagining of “another possibility” that arose from them. For him, self-mythologizing is not a narrative formed in retrospect, but a deliberate choice in his practice: through images, matter and narrative, he continually translates, shifts and reconfigures personal experience to construct a personal mythology that both draws on reality and intentionally departs from it. This process also forms his basic approach to the relationships between the self, memory, history and fiction. While studying at Hubei Science and Technology College, this longstanding inclination gradually developed into a more conscious artistic practice under the influence of artist Li Wen.
