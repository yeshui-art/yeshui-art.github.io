About the artist

Ye Shui (Yang Yi)
Based in Wuhan, China

Ye Shui is an interdisciplinary artist working across painting, mixed media, text and moving image. Through image translation and material intervention, he destabilizes images and matter through layering, sedimentation, erosion and destruction. His practice examines the formation and failure of selfhood, memory, relationships and reality.
