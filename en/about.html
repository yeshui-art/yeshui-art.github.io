About the artist

Ye Shui (Yang Yi)
Based in Wuhan, China

Ye Shui is an interdisciplinary artist working across painting, mixed media, text, photography and moving image. Taking the translation of experience as his basic method, he works between individual memory, collective psychological experience and fragments of history, allowing images, bodies and matter to become continually destabilized through layering, sedimentation, displacement and reconfiguration. His practice explores the self and its shadow, the formation and failure of relationships and reality, and repeatedly returns to how the subject affirms itself when myth, history and everyday experience are out of alignment with one another.

Ye Shui currently lives and works in Wuhan. His artistic practice was not formed through traditional fine arts academy training, but began with a sense of alienation from the world around him that he had felt since childhood. This gradually developed into a sustained concern with constructing a world of his own and, while he was studying at Hubei Science and Technology College, evolved into an ongoing artistic practice under the influence of artist Li Wen.

His education spans animation design and fashion design. This varied path has also kept his work moving between images, matter and narrative.

He works with painting, mixed media, photography, text and moving image, using materials such as ash, plant matter, metal foil, sand and soil, and found objects. Layering, washing, sedimentation, erosion and destruction are both material processes and ways of working with images and experience. In his texts, this method takes the form of a continual reconfiguration of memory, the body, dreams, history, relationships in reality and narrative structures. The figures in his works are usually not stable, complete subjects, but are closer to a threshold state between seeing and being seen, affirmation and failure, the self and its shadow.
