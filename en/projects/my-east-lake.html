My East Lake

2023–ongoingOngoing Photographic Project

Introduction

My East Lake is a long-term photographic project begun in 2023, continuously documenting different individuals as they face East Lake. The project grew out of my investigation into “Jump into East Lake” and related public art actions around the lake, as well as my own longstanding experience of this urban space. Over the years, the ways East Lake is developed, used and viewed have continued to change, while the lake remains part of urban life. The project does not seek to reenact the earlier “East Lake Project,” but returns to the same place in a more personal, gradual way: recording the changing relationship between people and East Lake through acts of looking, and how the city continues to pass by it in everyday life.
