Three Kinds of Beds

2026

Acrylic, bandages, iron powder, treated paper and mixed media on canvas

60 × 40 cm

First there is the bed itself, then the carpenter’s bed, and only then the painter’s bed. By the time it reaches us, how many times has reality been retold? When the carpenter’s bed and the painter’s bed begin to overlap, where do we find ourselves?
